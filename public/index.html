Elise's Cookin'

Personal Project / Web Design

A website project built from scratch. I researched competing recipe sites and defined a persona and target audience, then made wireframes to set the structure. A style tile gave the site its vibe and personality and settled the colors, typefaces and icons. I designed the pages and applied basic web and UX practices throughout, and the final is a detailed mockup with several pages coded in HTML and CSS.

Collage Collision

Collage / Packaging / Visual Metaphor

Collage Collision turns packaging into a visual metaphor for the product inside. Each icon is built from pieces of that product's own wrapper: a tomato from ketchup packets, a water drop from bottle labels, a flame from Flamin' Hot Cheetos bags, lips from Hershey's Kisses wrappers. The nine icons then collide into one composition.

Ink Art

I let the ink find its own path, then brought the drops into Photoshop and added digital touches. The result sets the random, organic drops against the technical precision of perfect circles, two contrasting elements in one piece.
