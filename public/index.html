Elise's Cookin'

Personal Project / Web Design

A recipe website. I researched competing recipe sites and defined a persona and target audience, then built wireframes in Illustrator to set the structure. Style tiles settled the colors, typefaces and icons. The final is a detailed mockup, with several pages coded in HTML and CSS.

Collage Collision

Collage / Packaging / Visual Metaphor

Collage Collision turns packaging into a visual metaphor for the product inside. Each icon is built from pieces of that product's own wrapper: a tomato from ketchup packets, a water drop from bottle labels, a flame from Flamin' Hot Cheetos bags, lips from Hershey's Kisses wrappers. The nine icons then collide into one composition.

About

I'm a graphic designer with 5+ years of experience creating digital, print and social media marketing materials for multi-location automotive dealerships. I earned a BFA in Graphic Design, with a minor in Interactive Media & Design, from West Virginia University.

My day-to-day is campaigns for OEM promotions, holidays and events across multiple brands, following each brand's guidelines while managing several projects on tight deadlines. Beyond ads, my experience includes branding, print design, web design and motion graphics. I like solving design challenges and turning ideas into clear, engaging visuals.
